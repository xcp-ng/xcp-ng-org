# Support & Subscription

XCP-ng is fully open source: **all its features are available for free**, with or without a subscription. A subscription buys professional support from the team that builds XCP-ng, it does not unlock anything in the hypervisor.

## Community support

Community support happens on the channels listed in [Community](project/about.md#community), with no guarantee of a solution or a response time. To get the best answers, follow the [3-Step-Guide](troubleshooting/troubleshooting.md#the-3-step-guide).

## Professional support

Professional support for XCP-ng comes with a **Vates VMS subscription**, which covers your whole stack: all your XCP-ng hosts, Xen Orchestra, and the support itself. Subscribers open tickets on the [Vates help desk](https://docs.vates.tech/support/open-support-ticket).

## Subscription details

Everything commercial lives in one central place, the [Vates VMS docs](https://docs.vates.tech/), so it is always current:

- [Vates VMS bundle overview](https://docs.vates.tech/pricing-licencing/vms-bundle-overview/): what each subscription includes.
- [Pricing and available add-ons](https://docs.vates.tech/pricing-licencing/pricing-addons/): including XOSTOR and other add-ons.
- [Activating your subscription on your hosts](https://docs.vates.tech/pricing-licencing/applying-xcpng-licences/): bind the support of your subscription to your XCP-ng hosts from Xen Orchestra.
- [Contact and quote requests](https://docs.vates.tech/pricing-licencing/contact-quote-requests/): for purchase departments and quotes.
